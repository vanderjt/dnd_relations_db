PHASE 02 · INTERACTIVE PROTOTYPE

A new chapter for Story Atlas.

A focused place to develop one character at a time, through the events of Greyhaven.

Try it: choose a character, edit a few fields, then review which changes carry forward. Move along the timeline to see the result.

Greyhaven supplies all 18 characters, 3 chapters, 10 events and the original relationship history. Character profiles are template starting points, not reconstructed historical profiles. Unspecified ages and statistics stay blank.

Saved prototype edits stay in this browser only. They do not change a Story Atlas database. Unsubmitted edits prompt before navigation. Use Ctrl/Cmd + S to review changes, or / to find a character.

This study explores layout and interaction. Portraits, the final visual theme and the event-creation workspace are still open design decisions.

Return to the original template?
